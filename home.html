Who we are

The voice of the security industry in Region 8

We represent locally based private security agencies in Eastern Visayas: businesses that hire local guards, pay local taxes, and answer to local communities. Our members commit to full licensing, fair labor practices, and close coordination with regulators.

We work alongside the PNP Supervisory Office for Security and Investigation Agencies (SOSIA), the Regional Civil Security Unit 8 (RCSU 8), and the Department of Labor and Employment Region 8 (DOLE 8).

Our advocacy

Priority agenda for 2026

These priorities guide the association's work with regulators, local officials and members.

Local-First Procurement Policy

Through consultative meetings with Board Members and District Representatives, we are pushing for a policy that prioritizes local businesses, especially security agencies based in the region.

Ending Non-Compliant Operations

A joint effort with PNP RCSU 8 and DOLE Region 8 to address non-compliant players in the security industry, with formal letters of coordination to both offices.

Protecting the Local Economy

  • Ensuring taxes are remitted to local revenue districts
  • Preserving livelihood for locally qualified security personnel

Licensing & Regulatory Readiness

Helping members stay current with licensing and regulatory requirements under the Private Security Services Industry Act (RA 11917) and PNP SOSIA rules.
