Who we are

The voice of the security industry in Region 8

We represent locally based private security agencies in Eastern Visayas: businesses that hire local guards, pay local taxes, and answer to local communities.

Our government partners
  • PNP SOSIA — licensing and supervision
  • PNP RCSU 8 — regional regulation
  • DOLE Region 8 — labor standards for security personnel
  • Local government — Board Members and District Representatives

Our advocacy

Priority agenda for 2026

Tap a priority to see what we're doing about it.

Local-First Procurement Policy

Through consultative meetings with Board Members and District Representatives, we are pushing for a policy that prioritizes local businesses, especially security agencies based in the region.

Ending Non-Compliant Operations

A joint effort with PNP RCSU 8 and DOLE Region 8 to address non-compliant players in the security industry, starting with formal letters of coordination to both offices.

Protecting the Local Economy
  • Ensuring taxes are remitted to local revenue districts
  • Preserving livelihood for locally qualified security personnel
Licensing & Regulatory Readiness

Helping members stay current with licensing requirements under the Private Security Services Industry Act and PNP SOSIA rules.

Who can join?

Owners and authorized officers of private security or detective agencies with a valid License to Operate and operations in Region 8.

What do I need to prepare?
  • Agency's current License to Operate (LTO)
  • Business registration documents
  • Contact person and position in the agency
How long does approval take?

Applications are reviewed by the officers, usually at or before the next general meeting. You can log in anytime to check your status.
